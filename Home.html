Features

Everything for the chair, nothing for the meeting room.

Most salon software is built for chains with twelve stylists. This one's built for you, alone, between baths.

Calendar that thinks ahead

Drag to reschedule. Color blocks by service. Buffer time between dogs auto-baked in. See your whole week without losing today.

AI Receptionist

Picks up the phone, checks your real availability, books appointments, and texts confirmations. While you're elbow-deep in suds.

Square payments, built in

Connect your Square account once. Tap, dip, or Tap-to-Pay on the iPhone you already own. Invoices auto-match the booking.

Re-booking on autopilot

Knows that Bella is due every six weeks. Surfaces the dogs slipping past their cadence. One tap to text and book.

A real client memory

Allergies, behaviors, the specific shampoo they like. Photos before and after. Every dog, every owner, every preference — right where you'd look first.

Money, plain and simple

Today, this week, this month — what came in, what's outstanding, what your average ticket looks like. No spreadsheets, no surprises.

How it works

From "I should get organized" to booked solid for May.

01

Bring your clients

Drop in a CSV, type them in, or let the AI grab them off voicemails. Whatever's least painful.

02

Set your week

Hours, services, prices, time between dogs. Block off lunch and a real coffee break. The calendar respects all of it.

03

Just groom

Bookings come in. Reminders go out. Payments settle. You touch the app twice a day and it's still ahead of you.
